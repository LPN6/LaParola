Psalm 134
1 A SONG OF THE ASCENTS. Behold, bless YHWH, all servants of YHWH, || Who are standing in the house of YHWH by night. 2 Lift up your hands [in] the sanctuary, || And bless YHWH. 3 YHWH blesses you out of Zion, || The Maker of the heavens and earth!
