Psalm 100
1 A PSALM OF THANKSGIVING. Shout to YHWH, all the earth! 2 Serve YHWH with joy, come before Him with singing. 3 Know that YHWH [is] God, || He made us, and we are His, || His people—and the flock of His pasture. 4 Enter His gates with thanksgiving, || His courts with praise, || Give thanks to Him, bless His Name. 5 For YHWH [is] good, || His kindness [is] for all time, || And His faithfulness to generation and generation!
