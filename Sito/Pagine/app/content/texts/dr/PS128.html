Psalm 128
1 Often have they fought against me from my youth, let Israel now say. 2 Often have they fought against me from my youth: but they could not prevail over me. 3 The wicked have wrought upon my back: they have lengthened their iniquity. 4 The Lord who is just will cut the necks of sinners: 5 let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Sion. 6 Let them be as grass on the tops of houses: which withered before it be plucked up: 7 Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand: nor he that gathereth sheaves his bosom. 8 And they that have passed by have not said: The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we have blessed you in the name of the Lord.
