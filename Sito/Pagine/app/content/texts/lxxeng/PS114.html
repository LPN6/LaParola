Psalm 114
1 {\i Alleluia.} - At the going forth of Israel from Egypt, of the house of Jacob from a barbarous people, 2 Judea became his {Or, consecrated thing} sanctuary, and Israel his dominion. 3 The sea saw and fled: Jordan was turned back. 4 The mountains skipped like rams, and the hills like lambs. 5 What ailed thee, O sea, that thou fleddest? and thou Jordan, that thou wast turned back? 6 Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams, and ye hills, like lambs? 7 The earth trembled at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob; 8 who turned the rock into pools of water, and the {Gr. sharp rock} flint into fountains of water.
