Psalm 128
1 {\i A Song of Degrees.} - Blessed are all they that fear the Lord; who walk in his ways. 2 Thou shalt eat the {Alex. fruits of thy labours} labours of thy hands: blessed art thou, and it shall be well with thee. 3 Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine on the sides of thy house: thy children as young olive-plants round about thy table. 4 Behold, thus shall the man be blessed that fears the Lord. 5 May the Lord bless thee out of Sion; and mayest thou see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of thy life. 6 And mayest thou see thy children's children. Peace be upon Israel.
