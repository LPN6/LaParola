Psalm 93
1 {\i For }{Alex. the sabbath-day}{\i the day before the Sabbath, when the land was first inhabited, the praise of a Song by David.} - The Lord reigns; he has clothed himself with honour: the Lord has clothed and girded himself with strength; for he has established the world, which shall not be moved. 2 Thy throne is prepared of old: thou art from everlasting. 3 The rivers have lifted up, O Lord, the rivers have lifted up their voices, {Alex. +the floods will lift up their waves; See Heb.} 4 at {q. d. in answer to} the voices of many waters: the billows of the sea are wonderful: the Lord is wonderful in high places. 5 Thy testimonies are made very sure: holiness becomes thine house, O Lord, {Gr. to length of days} for ever.
