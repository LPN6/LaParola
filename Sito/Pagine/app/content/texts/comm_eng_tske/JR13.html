Jeremiah 13
Overview
13:1 By the type of a linen girdle, hidden at Euphrates, God prefigures the destruction of his people;
13:12 Under the parable of the bottles filled with wine he foretells their drunkenness in misery;
13:15 He exhorts to prevent their future judgments;
13:22 He shews their abominations are the cause thereof.
