Overview

37:6 Jeremiah prophesies the Chaldeans' certain return and victory;
37:11 He is taken for a fugitive, beaten, and put in prison;
37:16 He assures Zedekiah of the captivity;
37:18 Intreating for his liberty, he obtains some favour.
