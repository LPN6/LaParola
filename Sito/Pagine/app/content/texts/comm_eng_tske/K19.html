Judges 14:3 *marg.Reciprocal1 Kings 5:1 - Hiram Verse 13my brother1 Kings 5:1, 5:2; Amos 1:9 Cabulthat is, Displeasing, or dirty. Josephus says that Cabul, in the Phoenician language, signifies ουκ αρεσκον, displeasing; and that these cities were situated in the neighbourhood of Tyre. Most commentators are persuaded that the city Cabul in the tribe of Asher was one; and probably from this Hiram took occasion to give this name to all the other cities which Solomon had ceded to him.
