Overview

Verse 1am 2962, bc 1042 TitleIt appears from 1Chr. 16, where the former part of this Psalm, as far as the Psalm 105:16, is found with little variation, that David composed it at the removal of the ark to Mount Zion, and he himself probably enlarged it afterwards with the glorious detail of God's merciful dealings with Abraham and his posterity till their settlement in the land of Promise.
