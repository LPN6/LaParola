Psalm 118
Overview
118:1 An exhortation to praise God for his mercy;
118:5 The psalmist by his experience shews how good it is to trust in God;
118:19 Under the type of the psalmist the coming of Christ in his kingdom is expressed. Verse 1am 2962, bc 1042 TitleThis Psalm was probably composed by David after Nathan's prophetic address; and sung by alternate choirs at some public festival. It largely partakes of David's spirit, and everywhere shews the hand of a master; the style is grand and sublime; the subject noble and majestic.
