2 Kings 7
Overview
7:1 Elisha prophesies incredible plenty in Samaria;
7:3 Four lepers, venturing on the host of the Syrians, bring tidings of their flight;
7:12 The king, finding by spies the news to be true, spoils the tents of the Syrians;
7:17 The lord who would not believe the prophecy of plenty, having the charge of the gate, is trodden to death in the press.
