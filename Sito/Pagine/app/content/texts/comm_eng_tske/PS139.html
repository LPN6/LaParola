Psalm 139
Overview
139:1 David praises God for his all-seeing providence;
139:17 and for his infinite mercies;
139:19 He defies the wicked;
139:23 He prays for sincerity.

This Psalm is supposed to have been composed by David when made king of Israel; though some think it was written by him when accused of traitorous designs against Saul. It is a most sublime ode on the wisdom, knowledge, presence, and justice of God. the sentiments are grand, the style highly elevated, and the images various, beautiful, and impressive.
