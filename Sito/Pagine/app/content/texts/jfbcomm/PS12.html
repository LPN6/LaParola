Psalm 12
1 Psa 12:1-8. On title, see on Introduction and see on Psa 6:1. The Psalmist laments the decrease of good men. The pride and deceit of the wicked provokes God's wrath, whose promise to avenge the cause of pious sufferers will be verified even amidst prevailing iniquity.
the faithful - or literally, "faithfulness" (Psa 31:23).

 2 The want of it is illustrated by the prevalence of deceit and instability.

 3 Boasting (Dan 7:25) is, like flattery, a species of lying.
lips, and ... tongue - for persons.

 5 The writer intimates his confidence by depicting God's actions (compare Psa 9:19; Psa 10:12) as coming to save the poor at whom the wicked sneer (Psa 10:5).

 6 The words - literally, "saying of" (Psa 12:5).
seven times - thoroughly (Dan 3:19).

 7 them - (Margin.)

 8 The wicked roam undisturbed doing evil, when vileness and vile men are exalted.
