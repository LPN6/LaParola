Psalm 121
1 Psa 121:1-8. God's guardian care of His people celebrated.
I will lift up mine eyes - expresses desire (compare Psa 25:1), mingled with expectation. The last clause, read as a question, is answered,

 2 by avowing God to be the helper, of whose ability His creative power is a pledge (Psa 115:15), to which,

 3 His sleepless vigilance is added.
to be moved - (Compare Psa 38:16; Psa 66:9).

 5 upon thy right hand - a protector's place (Psa 109:31; Psa 110:5).

 6 God keeps His people at all times and in all perils.
nor the moon by night - poetically represents the dangers of the night, over which the moon presides (Gen 1:16).

 8 thy going out, etc. - all thy ways (Deu 28:19; Psa 104:23).
evermore - includes a future state.
