Psalm 53
1 Psa 53:1-6. Upon Mahalath - (See on Psa 88:1, title). Why this repetition of the fourteenth Psalm is given we do not know.
with few verbal changes, correspond with Psa 14:1-4.

 5 Instead of assurances of God's presence with the pious, and a complaint of the wicked, Psa 14:5, Psa 14:6 portrays the ruin of the latter, whose "bones" even "are scattered" (compare Psa 141:7), and who are put to shame as contemptuously rejected of God.
