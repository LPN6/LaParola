Psalm 71
1 Psa 71:1-24. The Psalmist, probably in old age, appeals to God for help from his enemies, pleading his past favors, and stating his present need; and, in confidence of a hearing, he promises his grateful thanks and praise.
(Compare Psa 30:1-3).

 3 given commandment - literally, "ordained," as in Psa 44:4; Psa 68:28.
rock ... fortress - (Psa 18:2).

 4 cruel man - corrupt and ill-natured - literally, "sour."

 5 trust - place of trust.

 6 His history from early infancy illustrated God's care, and his wonderful deliverances were at once occasions of praise and ground of confidence for the future.
my praise ... of thee - literally, "in" or "by Thee" (Psa 22:25).

 10 The craft and malicious taunts of his enemies now led him to call for aid (compare the terms used, 2Sa 17:12; Psa 3:2; Psa 7:2).

 12 (Compare Psa 22:19; Psa 40:4).

 13 (Compare Psa 35:4; Psa 40:14).

 14 The ruin of his enemies, as illustrating God's faithfulness, is his deliverance, and a reason for future confidence.

 15 for I know ... thereof - innumerable, as he had not time to count them.

 16 in the strength - or, relying on it.
thy righteousness - or, faithful performance of promises to the pious (Psa 7:17; Psa 31:1).

 17 Past experience again encourages.
taught me, etc. - by providential dealings.

 19 is very high - distinguished (Psa 36:5; Isa 55:9).

 20 depths of the earth - debased, low condition.

 21 increase, etc. - that is, the great things done for me (Psa 71:19; compare Psa 40:5).

 22 To the occasion of praise he now adds the promise to render it.
will ... praise - literally, "will thank."
even thy truth - as to Thy truth or faithfulness.
