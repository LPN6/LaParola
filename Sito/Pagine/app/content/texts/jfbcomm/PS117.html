Psalm 117
1 Psa 117:1, Psa 117:2. This may be regarded as a doxology, suitable to be appended to any Psalm of similar character, and prophetical of the prevalence of God's grace in the world, in which aspect Paul quotes it (Rom 15:11; compare Psa 47:2; Psa 66:8).

 2 is great toward us - literally, "prevailed over" or "protected us."
