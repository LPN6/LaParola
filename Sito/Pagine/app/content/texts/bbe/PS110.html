Psalm 110
1  The Lord said to my lord, Be seated at my right hand, till I put all those who are against you under your feet. 2  The Lord will send out the rod of your strength from Zion; be king over your haters. 3  Your people give themselves gladly in the day of your power; like the dew of the morning on the holy mountains is the army of your young men. 4  The Lord has made an oath, and will not take it back. You are a priest for ever, after the order of Melchizedek. 5  In the day of his wrath kings will be wounded by the Lord at your right hand. 6  He will be judge among the nations, the valleys will be full of dead bodies; the head over a great country will be wounded by him. 7  He will take of the stream by the way; so his head will be lifted up.
