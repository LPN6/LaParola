Psalm 123
1  To you my eyes are lifted up, even to you whose seat is in the heavens. 2  See! as the eyes of servants are turned to the hands of their masters, and the eyes of a servant-girl to her owner, so our eyes are waiting for the Lord our God, till he has mercy on us. 3  Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us: for all men are looking down on us. 4  For long enough have men of pride made sport of our soul.
