Psalm 128
1  Happy is the worshipper of the Lord, who is walking in his ways. 2  You will have the fruit of the work of your hands: happy will you be, and all will be well for you. 3  Your wife will be like a fertile vine in the inmost parts of your house: your children will be like olive plants round your table. 4  See! this is the blessing of the worshipper of the Lord. 5  May the Lord send you blessing out of Zion: may you see the good of Jerusalem all the days of your life. 6  May you see your children's children. Peace be on Israel.
